## Introduction

PT Global Prima Utama ("we", "our", or "us") operates the ODOO GPU mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our App.

## Information We Collect

### 1. Authentication Data

- Work email address
- Employee PIN

These credentials are used solely for authenticating with our internal HR system and are stored securely on your device using encrypted storage.

### 2. Location Data

- GPS coordinates (latitude and longitude)

Location data is collected **only** at the moment you perform a check-in or check-out action. We do not track your location continuously or in the background.

### 3. Network Information

- Public IP address
- Local IP address

Collected at the time of attendance action for verification purposes.

### 4. Device Information

- Device model and manufacturer
- Operating system version

Collected at the time of attendance action for audit logging.

## How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information exclusively for:

- Authenticating employees within our HR system
- Recording attendance check-in and check-out events
- Verifying attendance location for compliance purposes
- Maintaining audit logs for HR administration

## Data Storage and Security

- Authentication credentials are stored locally on your device using platform-native encrypted storage (Android Keystore / iOS Keychain).
- Attendance data (timestamps, location, device info) is transmitted securely via HTTPS to our middleware server and stored in our Odoo 18 Enterprise system.
- We do not store your PIN on our servers; it is used only for real-time authentication.

## Data Sharing

We do **not**:

- Sell your personal data to third parties
- Share your data with advertising networks
- Transfer your data to external analytics services

Your data is only accessible to authorized HR administrators within PT Global Prima Utama.

## Data Retention

Attendance records are retained in accordance with Indonesian labor regulations and our company's internal policies. You may request access to or deletion of your data by contacting your HR department.

## Your Rights

You have the right to:

- Access your attendance data through the App
- Request correction of inaccurate data
- Request deletion of your data (subject to legal retention requirements)
- Withdraw consent for location access (note: this will prevent attendance functionality)

## Permissions Required

| Permission | Purpose | When Used |
|---|---|---|
| Location (GPS) | Record attendance coordinates | Only during check-in/check-out |
| Internet | Communicate with HR system | During all app operations |

## Children's Privacy

This App is intended for employees of PT Global Prima Utama only and is not directed at children under the age of 13.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be communicated through the App or via internal company channels. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
